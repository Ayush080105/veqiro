Which aspect ratio?

Twitter/X and Instagram support all three — each has different reach and visual weight

9:16

A — 9:16 Vertical (1080×1920)

Takes over the full phone screen. Dominates Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Twitter mobile feeds. Maximum visual impact. This is where social media lives now.

Best for: Instagram Reels, Twitter mobile, Stories

1:1

B — 1:1 Square (1080×1080)

Works everywhere — Twitter feed, Instagram feed, LinkedIn. Not as immersive as vertical but plays natively on every platform without letterboxing. Safe universal choice.

Best for: Instagram feed, Twitter web + mobile, universal

16:9

C — 16:9 Widescreen (1920×1080)

Classic video format. Shows as a thumbnail on Twitter/X feed (not full-bleed). Better for desktop viewers. Also doubles as a YouTube-ready export if you ever want that.

Best for: Twitter desktop, YouTube repurpose
