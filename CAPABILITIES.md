# Agent Capabilities Reference

One-liner descriptions for every capability card on each agent page.

## Vega — The Executive Assistant
*Your right hand, 24/7*

| Capability | One-liner |
|---|---|
| **Inbox Processing** | Reads 100 emails at once, labels by priority, and queues replies — one run. |
| **Reply Drafting** | Writes replies in your voice with the exact tone you set. |
| **Calendar Summary & Event Creation** | 30-day look-ahead, conflict detection, and natural-language event creation. |
| **Executive Briefing** | Morning digest: what's urgent, what she handled, what needs your eyes. |
| **Email Composition** | New email from scratch — recipient, topic, tone. Written and ready. |

## Cross-Agent Handoffs

Every agent can call another. Common handoff one-liners:

| From | To | One-liner |
|---|---|---|
| Scout trend | Maya | Turn a market signal into a ready-to-post draft. |
| Rex digest | Maya | Celebrate a financial milestone publicly in one click. |
| Rex investor update | Vega | Send the drafted investor email without leaving the platform. |
| Lex contract risk | Vega | Alert stakeholders about contract red flags instantly. |
| Sage keyword | Maya | Turn a ranked keyword into a social post idea. |
| Scout company profile | Maya | Counter-position against a competitor in one draft. |
