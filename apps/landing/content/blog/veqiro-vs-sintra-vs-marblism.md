The AI employee category has stopped being a curiosity. Founders and lean teams are now making real purchasing decisions between platforms that all claim to automate their business. The problem is most comparisons are written by one of the platforms being compared.

This one isn't. Here's how Veqiro, Sintra, and Marblism actually stack up across the features that matter.

## The Short Version

If you need a full specialist crew across multiple business functions — **Veqiro**. If you're primarily a solopreneur focused on social content — **Sintra**. If you need EA and content tools with some SEO coverage — **Marblism**. The right choice depends entirely on what your business actually needs covered.

## Executive Assistant: Comparable at the Core, Veqiro Wins on Depth

All three platforms offer executive assistant capabilities — inbox management, email drafting, calendar management, and meeting scheduling. At the surface level, they're similar.

The differences show up in depth.

Veqiro's Vega goes further: auto email draft queues, VIP contact prioritization, smart email labeling, and natural language AI event creation. Sintra and Marblism cover the basics — read, draft, schedule — but both label more advanced inbox features as "Basic" or don't offer them at all.

> "Your EA should proactively handle the inbox, not just respond when asked."

For founders getting 200+ emails a week, the gap between basic inbox management and a full AI EA with auto-draft queues is the difference between reclaiming time and just having a slightly smarter interface.

**Winner: Veqiro** — deeper feature set at every level of executive support.

## Research & Intelligence: Veqiro Stands Alone

This is the clearest gap in the comparison.

Veqiro's Scout is a dedicated research agent: live-source topic research, company research, competitor research with auto-discovery, a competitor watchlist, and trending topics tracking.

Marblism has no research agent. None.

Sintra has limited research capabilities — some competitor research and basic topic tracking, but no auto-discovery, no watchlist, and no deep company research capability.

For founders running competitive markets, this isn't a nice-to-have. Understanding what competitors are doing, tracking industry shifts, and generating research-backed content briefs are weekly tasks that consume hours without a dedicated agent.

**Winner: Veqiro** — the only platform with a full research and intelligence agent.

## SEO: All Three Have It — Depth Varies Significantly

All three platforms include an SEO agent or equivalent. But the coverage levels are materially different.

Veqiro's Sage covers: keyword research, blog generation, SEO page audits, **website-level SEO audits** (full-site analysis — neither Sintra nor Marblism offer this), content analysis and page scoring, content brief generation, and blog idea saving with keyword tracking.

Sintra has strong keyword research and blog generation, with limited SEO page audit capabilities. No website-level SEO audit, no content brief generator.

Marblism has basic keyword research and blog generation, but no SEO audits of any kind, no content analysis, and no content brief generation.

The website-level SEO audit is the most significant gap. Knowing which individual keywords to target is one thing — understanding your full domain's SEO health is another. Only Veqiro does the latter.

**Winner: Veqiro** — fullest SEO coverage. Sintra second. Marblism weakest.

## Content & Marketing: Strongest Common Ground

This is where the platforms are most comparable, because content is where all three started.

All three offer: full content drafting, direct social publishing, cross-platform content variants, and AI image generation.

Veqiro goes further with AI image regeneration (iterate on generated images), product campaign creators, and carousel post generation — neither Sintra nor Marblism offer these fully.

Sintra remains strong for high-volume social posting. If you're publishing 15+ times per week across platforms and content volume is your primary metric, Sintra's social-first design has been optimised for exactly this.

Veqiro's Maya is more of an all-purpose content strategist than a social posting engine — better for campaigns, launches, and content with depth, not just volume.

**Winner: Tied for core content.** Veqiro ahead on campaign tools. Sintra ahead on social volume workflow.

## Legal: Only Two Platforms Have This Function

Sintra has no legal coverage. If you need contract review, compliance research, or legal document drafting, Sintra simply isn't built for it.

Marblism offers partial legal coverage — AI legal assistant, contract analysis, and legal document drafting are available, but contract Q&A, compliance research, and document library features are limited or basic.

Veqiro's Lex is a full legal assistant: contract analysis, contract Q&A, plain English legal explanations, compliance research, legal document drafting, letterhead stamping on PDFs, and a full document library with search.

For founders signing NDAs, reviewing vendor contracts, or navigating employment agreements — legal AI is a real-world use case that runs weekly. The difference between "we have a legal AI" and "we have Lex" is the difference between a general-purpose chatbot and a trained specialist.

**Winner: Veqiro** — Marblism second. Sintra has nothing here.

## Finance: The Biggest Gap in the Market

This is where the comparison becomes stark.

Sintra has a financial analyst capability with some financial metric tracking. Marblism has none.

Veqiro's Rex covers: revenue forecasting, financial KPI dashboards, runway and unit economics analysis, scenario modeling, investor update generation, board deck creation, weekly business digests, CSV/Excel upload with natural language querying, and AI report generation.

The finance gap isn't a missing feature — it's a missing category. For any startup tracking MRR, monitoring burn rate, or preparing for investor meetings, this is a weekly job. The fact that only one of these three platforms covers it at all is the most important finding in this comparison.

**Winner: Veqiro** — the only platform with a dedicated financial analyst agent.
